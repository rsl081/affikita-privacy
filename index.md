# AffiKita Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

AffiKita is provided by **One-Man IT Department**. This policy explains how the
current Android app handles information when you organize affiliate products,
plan content, and manually track performance, commissions and expenses.

For privacy questions or app support, contact
[oneman.it.department.support@gmail.com](mailto:oneman.it.department.support@gmail.com).

## Information you enter

AffiKita stores the records you choose to enter or import, including:

- Product names, prices, links, commission rates or per-sale estimates, statuses
  and product-selection checklist answers.
- Video titles, notes, product associations, platform links, planned posting dates
  and actual publication dates.
- Performance check dates, views, sales value (GMV) and items sold.
- Commission estimates, settled commissions, expenses and monthly goals.

The app also creates local record identifiers, timestamps and backup information
to manage your workspace. Free-text fields and links may contain personal
information if you choose to include it. Avoid entering passwords, payment-card
details or other unnecessary sensitive information.

## Where your workspace is stored and how it is used

Your workspace is stored in an on-device database. The app uses these records to
display your plans, calculate reports and totals, and provide backup and restore.
The current version does not require an AffiKita account or provide automatic
workspace synchronization to a developer-operated cloud service. One-Man IT
Department does not receive your workspace through normal local record keeping.

Android or your device manufacturer may separately back up or transfer app data,
depending on your device, account and backup settings. This can include cloud
backup or transfer to another device. Those services are controlled by their
providers and your device settings; they are separate from AffiKita's manual
backup feature. Consult your device's backup settings and the provider's policy.

## Backup files and sharing

When you export a backup, AffiKita writes a JSON file to the location you select
and retains an app-private copy for backup/recovery functions. Backup files can
include your complete workspace, including archived or previously removed records.
Restoring a backup replaces the current workspace after the safety-backup steps.

AffiKita does not encrypt its exported JSON backup files. Anyone with access to a
file may be able to read it. Choose where you save or share it carefully. A cloud
folder, email app or other destination you select handles the file under that
service's own terms and privacy practices. The app's sharing and file-selection
features use the files and destinations you choose.

## External links and clipboard

If you open a saved product or video link, your browser or the destination app
handles that visit under its own privacy policy. AffiKita does not sign in to
TikTok, Facebook, Shopee or other affiliate platforms for you or automatically
retrieve their performance or earnings data. The copy-link action writes the
selected link to your device clipboard.

## Advertising, analytics and Google Play services

The current version does not display advertisements or include a configured
third-party advertising or analytics service. We do not sell your workspace data.
If advertising or other data-handling features are added, we will update the
policy and relevant disclosures before those features are released.

Google Play and Android may process installation, update, security-check and
diagnostic information through their own services and settings. Play-distributed
versions may include Google's installer protection. These services are governed
by [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Support messages

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever message or attachments
you send. We use them to respond to questions, investigate reported problems and
handle privacy requests. Our email provider processes those communications.
Please send only the information needed to explain your issue; do not send your
full backup or private financial records unless necessary and specifically agreed.

We keep support correspondence for as long as reasonably needed to handle the
request, related follow-up or applicable legal obligations. You can contact us to
request deletion of support information you supplied. Any necessary retention
exceptions will be explained when responding to your request.

## Retention and deletion of local information

Workspace records remain in the app until changed, replaced or removed. Archiving
does not erase a record. Some removal actions mark a record as removed so that it
can be restored; its information may remain in the database and backups.

To remove the app's local workspace and app-private backup copies, use Android's
app settings to clear AffiKita's storage, or uninstall the app without keeping its
data. This also removes records you may want to keep, so first save any needed
backup elsewhere. Device behavior and settings may vary.

Clearing app storage or uninstalling does not delete backup files saved outside
the app, files already shared with others, or copies held by system/cloud backup
services. Delete those separately through their folders, apps or provider
settings. Restoring a device or importing an older backup may bring records back.
We cannot remotely inspect or erase records stored only on your device or in
destinations you control.

## Security

The app uses Android's app storage protections for its local database and private
files. Your device lock, operating-system security and backup destinations also
affect their protection. The app does not add a separate encrypted vault for the
database or exported backups. No storage or transmission method is completely
secure.

## Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app or its information practices change. The
updated policy will show its revision date. Material changes requiring notice or
consent will be handled before the relevant new processing begins.

For questions about this policy, support information or your privacy choices,
email **oneman.it.department.support@gmail.com** and identify **AffiKita** in your
message.
